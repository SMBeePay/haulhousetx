Good to know

Liquidation is a great value when you know what you are buying.

Are the items new?

Condition varies. Items are overstock, open-box, shelf pulls, or customer returns. Each listing describes what we know about that item.

Do you offer returns or warranties?

Items are sold as-is, which is how we keep prices low. Every sale happens in person, so you can inspect the item at pickup before you pay. Ask us anything beforehand. We want to build a long-standing business and real relationships with our fellow community members, so we will always work with you to make sure you are happy and well taken care of.

Do you ship?

No. We sell locally, with pickup in the Royse City area.

How can I pay?

We accept cash, Venmo, and Zelle. All sales are in person at pickup.
